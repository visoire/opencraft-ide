## The engines

WordCraft, GridCraft, DeckCraft and PdfCraft are developed in their own repositories:

- [github.com/storytold/wordcraft](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft)
- [github.com/storytold/gridcraft](https://github.com/storytold/gridcraft)
- [github.com/storytold/deckcraft](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft)
- [github.com/storytold/pdfcraft](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft)

Bugs inside an app belong there; bugs in how OpenCraft hosts, syncs or drives an app belong here.

## You

Everyone who reports a bug, tests on a new device, improves the docs or sends a pull request is part
of OpenCraft. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
